1. Who we are

YuGo ("we", "us") operates a commute-carpooling service for Greater Cairo that matches people who make the same trip into recurring, verified groups. This policy explains what personal data we handle and why. It applies to the YuGo app, our website and related support channels.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account, verify your identity and keep the community safe.
  • To match commutes, manage seats and groups, and calculate and settle route contributions.
  • To power chat, notifications and live trip tracking.
  • To investigate reports, no-shows and disputes, and to enforce our Terms.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, fix bugs and improve the service.
  • To contact you about the beta, your account or changes to our policies.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

4. Who we share it with

  • Other members: only what the product needs. Matches and group members see your first name, photo, rating, reliability, verification badges and trip details. Your phone number, ID documents and exact home address are not shown to other members.
  • Trusted contacts: trip details you choose to share with them.
  • Service providers who process data for us: cloud hosting, Google Maps Platform (maps and place search), Firebase (push notifications), Sentry (crash monitoring), SMS delivery, identity-verification and payment providers.
  • Authorities and legal needs when required by Egyptian law or to protect safety and rights.
  • Business changes such as a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

5. Location

We ask for location permission to show nearby places, place you on the map and, for drivers, to share live position during an active trip. You can change the permission in your device settings. Without it some features, such as live trip tracking, will not work.

6. Retention

We keep data while your account is active and as long as needed for the purposes above. Verification images are kept only as long as needed to verify and defend against fraud. When you delete your account we remove your name, photo, email, date of birth, saved places and trusted contacts straight away. Trip, payment, rating and report records are kept for legal, accounting, safety and dispute reasons, detached from you.

7. Your rights

You can access and correct your data in the app, withdraw location and notification permissions on your device, and ask us to delete your account and data. You may also ask for a copy of your data, object to certain processing or ask us to restrict it, subject to applicable law including Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 151 of 2020). To exercise a right, follow our account deletion page or email sahla.aao007@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit, secure storage for credentials on your device, access controls and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your phone and one-time codes private and tell us at once if you suspect misuse.

9. Children

YuGo is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will delete the data.

10. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It stores your language and theme choice in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new date here and notify you in the app for material changes. Questions? Email sahla.aao007@gmail.com or use our contact page.
