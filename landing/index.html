The problem

Commuting in Cairo shouldn't cost a salary.

Ride-hailing solved one-off trips. Nobody solved the commute you make every single day.

Today

  • Ride-hailing twice a day is unaffordable as a habit.
  • Public transport doesn't reach the new cities.
  • WhatsApp carpools: no verification, no recourse.
  • No-shows, silent route changes, vague money-splitting.

With YuGo

  • A transparent route contribution, split fairly.
  • Matched on real route overlap, walk and timing.
  • Every member ID-verified before riding.
  • Reliability scores and reports hold everyone accountable.
How it works

From your door to your desk in three steps.

Publish your commute

Set where you start, where you go, your time window and the days you travel. Drivers add their car and open seats.

Get matched

We rank matches by route overlap, walking distance, detour, timing, reliability and the atmosphere you prefer.

Try it, then join

Take a single trial ride. Like it? Lock in a recurring seat in the group and commute with the same verified people.

Features

Built for the commute, not the one-off ride.

Groups, not trips

A confirmed seat makes you part of a persistent commute group with its own rules (quiet mornings, A/C on, non-smoking, women-only), members, chat and shared history.

Trial ride first

Test the fit with one ride before committing to a recurring seat.

Fair route contribution

The route cost is split across confirmed passengers, with a visible breakdown.

Group chat

Coordinate pickups and changes in one place instead of a messy WhatsApp thread.

Reliability you can see

Every member carries a rating, trip count and reliability score. No-shows and late cancellations are recorded events, not something to argue about in a group chat.

Safety first

Safety isn't a settings page. It's the foundation.

We built identity and accountability into every step, because trust is the reason a carpool survives.

Identity verification

Government ID, workplace and vehicle registration checks for drivers.

Women-only matching

Restrict your matches and groups to women.

Pre-trip check-in

Confirm driver, vehicle and plate before you get in.

Trusted contacts

Share trip visibility with people you trust.

Reports and blocking

Report an incident with evidence, or block anyone, in a few taps.
