### How staff review the 5 items
| Item | Where | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| National ID, selfie | **`/admin/verifications`** (verification queue) | **Approve**, or **Ask for info** with a reason → `rejectionReason` on that item only (`action_needed`) |
| Driving licence, car registration, car details | **`/admin/drivers`** (driver applications) | **Car review:** approved seats (≥ 3), tier (Standard/Comfort), AC. Then **Approve** or **Reject with a reason** |

- Rejecting one item **never clears** the others (tested in `DriverChecklistTest`).
- Since F2-2, a driver can't be approved until the ID **and the selfie** are approved in the verification queue. The page says which is missing.
- Each decision → `driver_item_approved` / `driver_item_rejected` (with `itemKey`, `rejectionReason`) / `driver_verified` (F2-14).
- Every decision is in the audit log (`/admin/audit`).
