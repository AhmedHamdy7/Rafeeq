## ✅ Backend done: F2-6 Vehicle approvedSeats + Comfort tier (B20 · PROF-26, PROF-28, PAY-42)

**Already on `main`**: built in **W1** (merge #18, "automatic seat price, car review, driver capability"). Guide §4.4 (vehicle fields) and §4.5 (commute seats, price).

### On the vehicle (`GET /driver/vehicles`, and in the driver's commute)
| Field | Means |
|---|---|
| `approvedSeats` | Bookable seats with working belts, driver excluded, **≥ 3**, set by the admin. `null` until the car review |
| `tier` | `standard` · `comfort` (set by the admin) |
| `hasAc` | Checked by the admin |
| `seats` | What the driver typed. It does **not** limit anything |

### Rules
- A commute or a day can't offer more seats than `approvedSeats` → `422 COMMUTE_SEATS_EXCEED_APPROVED`.
- A car with `approvedSeats: null` can't publish or be priced → `409 VEHICLE_SEATS_NOT_APPROVED` ("Waiting for your car review").
- **Price** (P-1): route length × the per-km rate of the `tier` (Standard / Comfort) ÷ `max(approvedSeats, 3)`, plus commission, rounded to 5 EGP. `GET /commutes/{commute}/price` shows it read-only.
- **Search results** carry `vehicle.tier` and `vehicle.hasAc` (Comfort badge), confirmed again in DISCOVER-5.
