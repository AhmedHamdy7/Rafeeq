## ✅ Backend done: TRIPS-17 Read and edit the booking's pickup note (B-17)

**Merged to `main`** (commit "Her pickup note on a booking … (TRIPS-17)"). New endpoint, so the guide now counts 189. Guide §4.7.

### Read
**`pickupNote`** on every booking (`GET /bookings/{booking}`, `GET /my-bookings`, …): her note, or `null`. Her driver sees the same field on that booking.

### Edit
`PATCH /bookings/{booking}/pickup-note` with `{ "note": "I'll wait by the pharmacy" }`
- Up to **200** characters. `null` or `""` clears it. Returns the updated booking.
- Her booking only, while `PENDING` / `CONFIRMED`.

| Refusal | When |
|---|---|
| `422 CHAT_CONTACT_INFO_NOT_ALLOWED` | A phone, email or social handle in it (same rule as chat) |
| `409 CONFLICT` | The seat is no longer upcoming |
| `404 NOT_FOUND` | Not her booking (drivers can't edit) |
