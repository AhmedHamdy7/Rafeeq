## ✅ Backend done: F2-14 Driver verification notifications (PROF-23)

**Merged to `main`** in `29b9423` (commit `8353639`). Guide §4.14 type list updated.

| `type` | When | `data` |
|---|---|---|
| `driver_item_approved` | A reviewer approves the ID, selfie or licence | `itemKey` |
| `driver_item_rejected` | A reviewer sends one back | `itemKey`, `rejectionReason` |
| `driver_verified` | The application is approved. **Once per account.** Replaces `driver_documents_approved` | `driverProfileId` |
| `driver_item_expiring` | Licence within 30 days of expiry, once per date. Replaces `licence_expiring` | `itemKey`, `expiresAt` |

- **Driver only** (has a driver application or the driver capability). A passenger gets none (tested). Safety category, can't be switched off. No paths, URLs or ID/licence numbers in the payload.
- The car is decided with the application, so it's covered by `driver_verified`. A whole-application rejection still sends `driver_documents_rejected`.
