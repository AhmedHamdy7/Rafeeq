**Answer to "does it cancel upcoming trips?" — yes** (D-0018 Q-027, PAY-46). Any suspension, conduct or ban, cancels **every upcoming seat, inside the 16 h lock too, with no fee for anybody**. Her seats as a passenger go to the waiting list. As a driver, every published commute is paused (`pausedReason: account_suspended`), which cancels its days and puts the riders back on the waiting list. Lifting the hold gives none of it back; she resumes herself.

**Built (AUTH-19):**
- A **suspension** stays in the app: `nextStep` = `HOME` (from `/auth/me`, `/auth/otp/verify`, refresh). Every `GET` still works (trips are read-only); every write answers `403 ACCOUNT_SUSPENDED`.
- Only a **permanent ban** answers `RESTRICTED_ACCOUNT` (reads closed too). Staff choose it with a new "Permanent ban" checkbox on the Members page.
- `user.suspension` (on `/auth/me`) now carries the banner data:
```json
"suspension": { "caseNumber": "RF-482913", "reasonCode": "policy_breach",
  "suspendedAt": "…", "reviewDueAt": "…", "permanent": false,
  "appeal": { "canAppeal": true, "appealBy": "2026-11-03T18:00:00+00:00",
              "needsNewEvidence": false, "appeals": [] } }
```
- **New `POST /account/suspension/appeal`** `{ message (10–1000), evidence? (≤2000) }` → `201 {id, attempt, status: "pending", createdAt, resolvedAt}`. One within 48 h of the hold; a second **only with `evidence`**, within 48 h of the first being upheld. Refusals are `422 error.fields.appeal`: `APPEAL_PENDING` · `EVIDENCE_REQUIRED` · `ALREADY_APPEALED` · `APPEAL_WINDOW_CLOSED` · `PERMANENT_BAN`; `404` if she is not suspended. Staff decide on the Members page; overturned lifts the hold (`account_reinstated` notification).
